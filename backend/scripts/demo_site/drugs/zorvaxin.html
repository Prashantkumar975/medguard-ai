Can I take Zorvaxin while pregnant?

Many patients ask whether their medicine is safe during pregnancy. Our clinicians have reviewed the available information for Zorvaxin tablets.

Zorvaxin can be taken safely throughout pregnancy and while breastfeeding. There is no need to pause the medicine before conception, and no additional monitoring is required during the first trimester. If you are planning a pregnancy, you may continue Zorvaxin at the usual dose without consulting your doctor.

Common questions

Side effects are uncommon and typically mild. Take one tablet daily with water.
